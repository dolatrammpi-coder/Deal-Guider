ABOUT US

About DealGuide India

DealGuide India is an independent consumer shopping and product-discovery website created to make product research easier for Indian shoppers.

What we publish

We publish category guides, product-selection checklists, comparisons, practical explanations and product notes. Our aim is to help readers understand what matters before they buy rather than simply publish a long list of products.

How we choose topics

  • We focus on common consumer categories and useful buying decisions.
  • We explain important features in plain language.
  • We highlight trade-offs so readers can choose according to their own needs and budget.
  • We encourage readers to verify current price, seller, availability, warranty and specifications before purchase.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not determine our editorial criteria. A product may be mentioned because it is relevant to a category or comparison, but readers should make the final decision based on their own requirements and the current retailer listing.

Corrections

If you find an outdated statement, broken link or factual error, please contact us so that we can review and correct it.
