Editorial Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Our purpose

Our content is designed to help readers make better-informed consumer product decisions through clear explanations, comparisons and practical checklists.

How we structure product coverage

  • Start with the user's likely use case.
  • Identify the features that materially affect the decision.
  • Explain important trade-offs and limitations.
  • Point readers toward current retailer/manufacturer information for price, stock and specifications.

Reviews and ratings

We do not present invented customer reviews, fabricated testimonials or made-up purchase experiences as real. Where current retailer ratings are relevant, they should be verified from the live retailer listing before being displayed.

Product images and brand references

Visuals used for site presentation are original design assets or properly permitted materials. Product names and trademarks are used for identification and editorial context. Amazon-provided Product Advertising Content, when used after program acceptance, will be used only within the permissions and technical requirements of the Associates Program.

Affiliate relationships

Affiliate relationships are disclosed. They do not change the basic editorial criteria used to explain a product category or buying decision.

Corrections

Readers can report errors or outdated information at guiderdeal@gmail.com. We review correction requests and update content when appropriate.
